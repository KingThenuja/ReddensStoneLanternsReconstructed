## Overview

**Redden's Stone Lanterns Reconstructed** is a decorative mod that expands your landscaping options with various traditional stone lighting fixtures and pillars. Perfect for shrines, quiet garden paths, ancient temples, or rustic mountain fortresses, these blocks blend seamlessly into vanilla stone environments while adding incredible architectural depth.

---

## Features

### 🏮 Modular Design & Variations
- **Lit & Unlit States:** Every lantern variant comes in both active (glowing) and extinguished forms to help set the exact mood your build requires.
- **Mix-and-Match Structures:** Combine bases, slender pillars, medium support columns, and decorative tops to build custom multi-block lanterns of varying heights.
- **Diverse Silhouettes:** From tiny ground-level accent lights to massive multi-meter structural pillars, find the perfect size for any space.

### ⚒️ Survival Friendly & Optimized Recipes
- **Resource Efficient:** Crafting shouldn't be a chore. Skip complex recipes by using the **Stonecutter** to directly carve your raw materials into any lantern component or pillar variant instantly.
- **Basic Component Crafting:** Simply combine basic blocks like Cobblestone with a piece of Coal or Charcoal to create your core lantern or pillar blanks, then shape them as you please!
